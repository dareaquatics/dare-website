Mission statement
DARE Aquatics fosters a thriving community united by a commitment to excellence in competitive swimming.

We offer age-specific training that challenges and develops each swimmer to the best of their abilities, fostering a lifelong love for the sport and personal growth.

Four values guide how we train and how we treat each other:

  • Speed. Train faster. Race harder. Push every boundary in the water and beyond.
  • Strength. Build physical and mental fortitude that lasts a lifetime outside the pool.
  • Character. Integrity, sportsmanship, and community above all else.
  • Excellence. Not just in swimming. We prepare athletes for success in everything they do.
Safe Sport statement

DARE Aquatics follows USA Swimming's Safe Sport program and the Minor Athlete Abuse Prevention Policy (MAAPP). These rules apply to every coach, staff member, volunteer, and parent who works with our swimmers.

To report a concern:

  • If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.
  • U.S. Center for SafeSport: report online or call 833-587-7233.
  • USA Swimming Safe Sport: usaswimming.org/safe-sport
  • Talk to any DARE coach or board member, or use our contact form.
